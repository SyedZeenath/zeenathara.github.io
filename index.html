About Me

I didn’t pivot away from software — I evolved through it. Seven years building industrial-grade systems taught me that the most fascinating engineering happens where software meets the physical world: in machines that sense, decide, and act.

Today I’m completing an MSc in Robotics & Embedded AI at Maynooth University, channelling production software maturity into ROS2, computer vision, and robotic manipulation. What makes me different? I combine technical depth with deployment reality — I’ve spent 12 months onsite at Shell Amsterdam, conducted Site Acceptance Testing, and bridged customer requirements with technical architecture across energy sector clients.

I’m a Women Techmakers Ambassador, a MUSE Platinum Award recipient, and a GenAI Hackathon winner. I believe the best robotics engineers understand both algorithms and customer needs, technical implementation and deployment constraints, code quality and business value.
